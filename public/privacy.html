LoveConnect Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Process

LoveConnect processes temporary session identifiers, connection details and technical information needed to operate the service.

2. Camera and Microphone

Camera and microphone access is requested only after users choose to start a video chat and grant browser permission.

3. Video and Messages

Video and audio are exchanged through WebRTC. Text messages are relayed through our server. This starter does not intentionally store chat messages or record video.

4. Reports and Safety

Users can report inappropriate behaviour. Basic report records may be stored for moderation.

5. Advertising

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.
